Generative Portrait

April 2026 ~10 hours
Generative

Concentric rings are perturbed with controlled randomness to echo a portrait silhouette without storing literal facial geometry. Each restart seeds a new arrangement. The piece foregrounds beginShape(), vertex(), and closed loops for a print-like contour stack.
