Cellular Automaton

April 2026 ~7 hours
Pixels Generative
View source

A toroidal grid implements Conway's rules: birth when exactly three neighbors live, survival with two or three, otherwise death. Two buffers swap each step for clarity. Drawing uses filled rectangles per cell so you can later swap to loadPixels() for larger grids if needed.

  • 2D arrays, neighbor counts
  • rect(), frameRate()
