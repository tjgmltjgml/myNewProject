Generative Noise Physics Interactivity

The capstone combines a flow field built from layered noise with many small particles that follow smoothed velocity vectors. Motion wraps at the edges for continuous streams. It is a compact synthesis of vector motion, noise sampling, and additive drawing that can grow into richer UI, color ramps, or audio reactivity for a final critique.
