Project 02

Particle System

April 2026 ~6 hours
Physics Generative
View source

Hundreds of lightweight particles spawn with random velocities and gentle gravity, fading as they age. Vectors keep motion readable: each particle stores position and velocity as p5.Vector instances. Trails come from a translucent full-canvas rectangle each frame rather than clearing to solid black.

  • createVector(), vector math
  • random()
  • map() for alpha
