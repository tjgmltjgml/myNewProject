Project 09

3D Form Study

April 2026 ~12 hours
3D Generative
View source

A torus and box share a lit scene in WEBGL. When available, orbitControl() lets you drag to tumble the forms; otherwise the sketch falls back to a slow autorotation. Directional and ambient lights separate surface normals without introducing a custom shader—leaving room to grow into materials and GLSL later.

  • createCanvas(w, h, WEBGL)
  • torus(), box(), lights
  • orbitControl() when supported
