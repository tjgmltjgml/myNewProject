Project 04

Audio Visualizer

April 2026 ~10 hours
Audio Interactivity
View source

This sketch uses your microphone. Click start to enable audio input (browser permission required).

The sketch analyzes incoming audio with p5.FFT and maps spectral bins to bar heights. Hue shifts across frequency for a quick read of the spectrum. A deliberate click starts the mic so browsers treat it as a user gesture before initializing p5.AudioIn.

  • p5.AudioIn, p5.FFT, analyze()
  • map() for bar height and color
