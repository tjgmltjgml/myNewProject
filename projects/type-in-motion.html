Project 06

Type in Motion

April 2026 ~6 hours
Typography Physics Generative
View source

Each glyph in a short word carries its own velocity and responds to gravity and walls. This is a lightweight stand-in for richer kinetic systems that might use textBounds() for collision boxes or custom fonts loaded with createFont(). Reduced-motion users see a gentle sine drift instead of free physics.

  • text(), textSize()
  • createVector() for motion
