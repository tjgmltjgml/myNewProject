Project 01

Generative Landscape

April 2026 ~8 hours
Noise Generative
View source

This study layers one-dimensional noise samples across the vertical axis to suggest rolling terrain. By animating the noise space slowly, the landscape drifts as if viewed from above moving clouds. The sketch focuses on noise() and noiseSeed() for repeatable structure while keeping the drawing loop light for smooth playback in the browser.

  • noise(), noiseSeed()
  • beginShape() / vertex()
  • map()
